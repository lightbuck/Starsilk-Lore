The Resono

The Resono are Humans with cat-like features who hail from the Realm of Donato .

Between the years of 295 to 300 Donato's ecology collapsed due to the sudden spread of a disease that targeted all plant life. The Resono scattered threefold amongst the stars. The first, the largest group, moved to Forem, aided by the Petrichor alliance, which was in the process of settling upon the realm as well. The second group moved to The Galactic Federation, settling on the less populated worlds with the aid of the Federal Governement. The third group decided to live amongst the stars themselves, creating Star-Keeps and other manners of space stations.

The Petrichor Resono melded over time with the Alkean and Zangherian population of Forem, the three groups combining into a new a tight knit community.

The Resono who joined the Federation struggled to adapt to the customs and regulations of the Federation, but they found their place as experts of Manascripting, bringing it to the Federation for the first time.

Finally the resono who remained in space discovered they could easily siphon Mana, allowing them to create any resource they may ever need with Manascripting. This meant that most of these communities became insular, the only exception being the Nova knights.
